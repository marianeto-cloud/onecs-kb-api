Overview
This article explains how Saved Searches and Favourite Ads work on Standvirtual, including limits, notifications, and expiry rules.
Definitions
Saved Search: A search query saved by a user to receive notifications when new matching ads are published.
Favourite Ad: An ad bookmarked by a user for easy access at a later time.
