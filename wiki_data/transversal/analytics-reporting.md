# Overview

This article covers the Analytics & Reporting tools available in Salesforce. It explains how to access and use Reports and Dashboards to monitor team performance, track key metrics, and support data-driven decision-making across the CS team.

---

# Definitions

**Report:** A structured data view in Salesforce, equivalent to Zendesk Explore Queries. Reports allow you to filter, sort, and group data to answer specific business questions.

**Dashboard:** A compilation of Reports displayed in visual formats such as bar charts, tables, pie charts, and more. Dashboards provide a real-time overview of performance metrics, similar to Zendesk Dashboards.


---

# Procedures

## 1. Reports

Reports in Salesforce allow you to extract and filter specific data sets for analysis.

## 2. Dashboards

Dashboards are a compilation of Reports displayed in different visual formats. They can show data as bar charts, tables, pie charts, and more, providing a comprehensive view of team performance at a glance.

---

# FAQs

**Q: What is the difference between a Report and a Dashboard?**
A: A Report is a single structured data view. A Dashboard combines multiple Reports into visual components (charts, tables) in a single view.


**Q: What types of visual components can appear in a Dashboard?**
A: Dashboards can display data as bar charts, pie charts, tables, gauges, and more, depending on how they are configured.
