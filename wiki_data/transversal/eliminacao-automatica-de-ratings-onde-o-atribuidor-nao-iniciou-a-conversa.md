> Conteúdo sincronizado a partir do excerto devolvido pelo índice Confluence; página completa consultada durante a sincronização.

Rule: Invalidate all ratings were the rating giver is not the conversation starter.
Why do we need this rule? We detected a significant amount of cases where sellers rate buyers back:
• To submit also a negative rating when they received one - as revenge.
To submit a positive ratings when they received one and  it was agreed to rate the same back as a favour
