Information about the ONE CS Team

# Overview

The ONE Customer Service Portugal (ONE CS PT) team is responsible for directly supporting users of the OLX, Imovirtual and Standvirtual platforms in Portugal. Our mission is to ensure a quality experience, resolve queries and issues efficiently, and contribute to the continuous improvement of our services.
