Overview
This article provides CS agents with a foundational understanding of GDPR, personal data, data subject rights, and key data protection terminology. It covers what constitutes personal data, the roles defined by GDPR, the rights users have over their data, and how to handle requests related to cookies, CNPD, and DPO.
