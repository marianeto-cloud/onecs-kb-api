Guuru — Platform Overview & CS Agent Guide
Overview
Guuru is a peer-to-peer support platform that connects users with verified community experts (Guurus) for quick, real-time assistance.
For OLX, Guuru operates as a first-line chat support channel available 7 days a week, 24h. During OLX working hours, Guuru experts can escalate conversations to OLX CS agent
