This article covers access, search, and refund procedures for Adyen — the payment verification system used by OLX PT.
Overview
Adyen is the payment verification and refund system used by OLX PT for payments made via MBWAY, Multibanco, and Visa/Credit Card. This article covers how to access the Adyen portal, search for payments, and process refunds.
