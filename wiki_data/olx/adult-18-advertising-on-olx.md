# Overview

# How to Report to Adops

## 1. Reporting Process

* Send an email to adops@olx.com with CC to happinessteam@olx.com
* Include the link / URL of the advertising
* Attach a screenshot of the advertising

## 2. Email Template

When reporting, include the following details in your email:

* Subject line: +18 / Inappropriate advertising detected on OLX
* URL or link of the advertising (if available)
* Screenshot of the advertising
* Any other relevant details (placement, date detected, etc.)

**Important:** If the user does not provide the ad link and we are unable to identify it ourselves, we must ask the user for the information before escalating to Adops.


---


# FAQs

**Q: What should I do if the user cannot provide the link to the advertising?**
A: Ask the user for as much information as possible (e.g. which page/category the ad appeared in, approximate time, device used). If we still cannot identify the ad, escalate with the available details and note that the link was not provided by the user.


**Q: Does this process apply to ads placed directly by sellers on OLX?**
A: No. This process applies specifically to advertising placed via Google Adsense. For direct seller ads that violate OLX rules, follow the standard content moderation workflow.

**Q: Who do I CC on the email to Adops?**
A: Always CC happinessteam@olx.com in addition to sending the email to adops@olx.com.
