# Overview

This page provides guidance for CS agents on how to handle cases where users send abusive, offensive, sexually inappropriate, intimidating or threatening messages to other users or advertisers on the platform. It covers communication standards, DSA compliance requirements, escalation criteria, and the recommended template response.

## Rules


1. Account restrictions must be proportionate, duly reasoned, and accompanied by access to a review/appeal channel (DSA requirement).
2. Communications must not simply state "breach of Terms and Conditions" — they must explain the nature of the conduct without disclosing personal data or internal moderation details.
3. CS agents are not obliged to engage in prolonged or repetitive discussions once the user has been informed of the restriction reasons and the appeal channel.
4. Evidence must be preserved before any deletion or alteration.
5. If a message constitutes a concrete threat to life or physical integrity, the case must be escalated and may require reporting to competent authorities (Article 18 DSA).


---


# Definitions

**DSA (Digital Services Act):** EU regulation governing notice-and-action mechanisms, statements of reasons for moderation decisions, internal complaint-handling mechanisms and measures against platform misuse.

**Misuse of the platform:** Conduct that breaches the rules on safe, lawful and respectful use, including abusive, offensive, sexually inappropriate, intimidating or threatening messages.

**Statement of reasons:** A sufficiently clear and specific explanation of the essential reasons underlying a moderation or restriction decision, as required by the DSA.

# Procedures

## 1. How to Communicate a Restriction Decision

When communicating an account restriction to the user:

* Do **not** state only that the account was restricted "for breach of the Terms and Conditions"
* Explain that the decision results from a review of interactions associated with the account which breached the rules on safe, lawful and respectful use
* Specify the nature of the conduct: abusive, offensive, sexually inappropriate, intimidating or threatening messages
* Ensure the communication includes access to the applicable review or complaint channel

## 3. When CS May Close the Interaction

CS agents may limit further substantive replies and close the interaction when:

* The user has already been informed of the restriction reasons and the appeal channel
* The user merely disputes the restriction in generic terms
* The user denies the conduct without presenting new relevant information
* The user continues to engage in abusive or inappropriate communications

## 4. When to Escalate

**Escalate internally and consider reporting to competent authorities when the message may constitute a concrete threat to life or physical integrity (Article 18 DSA), or when the conduct may have criminal relevance: threats, stalking, sexual harassment, or insult.**

## 5. Evidence Preservation

Before any deletion or alteration, preserve:

* Full content of the messages
* Date and time
* Account ID and Listing ID
* Identification of the affected advertiser
* Channel used
* Relevant account history

## 6. Account Measures to Apply


* **Maintain the restriction** if confirmed abusive/threatening messages were sent
* **Prevent further contact** between the user and the advertiser (if functionality available)
* **Record internally** that the measure was applied due to T&C breach, platform misuse and security/user protection reasons
* **Communicate the decision** objectively — freedom of expression does not legitimise abusive, offensive or threatening messages

# Common Problems

### Scenario 1: "User disputes the restriction claiming it is unfair"

**Solution:** Confirm the user has been informed of the restriction reasons and the appeal channel. If no new relevant information is provided, CS may close the interaction. Do not engage in prolonged discussion about the merits of the moderation decision.

# FAQs

**Q: Do we have to keep responding if the user keeps contesting the restriction?**
A: No. Once the user has been clearly informed of the reasons and provided access to the appeal channel, CS may close the interaction if no new relevant information is presented.

**Q: What if the message could be a criminal threat?**
A: Escalate internally. Depending on the wording and context, consider reporting to competent authorities under Article 18 DSA.

**Q: What template should I use to communicate the restriction?**
A: Use the template below.

# Template Response to User

Hello,

Your account has been restricted due to a breach of our Terms and Conditions, in particular the rules relating to the safe, lawful and respectful use of the platform.

The platform does not allow abusive, offensive, sexually inappropriate, intimidating or threatening messages directed at other users, advertisers or members of the OLX team.

The measure applied results from the review of interactions associated with your account and is intended to protect the safety and proper use of the platform by all users.

Should you wish to challenge this decision, you may submit a review request through [form / Help Centre / user account area / applicable email address].

We reserve the right to take any additional measures, including reporting the matter to the competent authorities, where the messages sent may constitute a threat or other unlawful conduct.


If you have already submitted a review request and no new relevant information is provided, the restriction decision may be maintained and no further substantive responses may be issued regarding this matter.


Thank you.
