# Overview

This article explains how to handle requests from customers who want to be put in contact with another OLX department or person (e.g. Marketing, Finance).

---

# Procedure

Whenever a customer contacts us asking for the email address of another department (e.g. Marketing, Finance), collect the following information before escalating:


* Full name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Brief summary of the matter

Reply to the customer letting them know that their request has been forwarded internally, and that they should expect to be contacted shortly.

**Note:** A comment must be added under **"chatter / post"** in the case, and it should be sent as **Solved** to the email address of the department or person who will follow up with the customer.
