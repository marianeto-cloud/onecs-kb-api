Overview
The mini-site is a major benefit when purchasing an ad package. It gives the user an exclusive space on the platform where their ads and business/company info appear. This article covers how to configure the mini-site, the Search ads in mini-site feature, and the Recommended Ads feature — all available to premium professional users.
