# Overview

This article explains how to handle requests from users who want to purchase banner advertising space or buy traffic on the OLX website.

---

# Procedure

Whenever a user contacts us to express interest in purchasing a banner, advertising space, or traffic on OLX, direct them to the following Help Center article:

[https://help.olx.pt/hc/pt/articles/360002775377-Publicidade-com-Banners](https://help.olx.pt/hc/pt/articles/360002775377-Publicidade-com-Banners)

For purchases, questions, or any other enquiries, interested parties should contact the AdSales team directly via email: adsales@olx.com

**Note:** If a purchase request is received via Salesforce, it can be forwarded directly to: adsales@olx.com

---

# Additional Information

The link [https://publicidade.olx.pt/](https://publicidade.olx.pt/) is currently not operational. When a new tool or website becomes available, it will be shared by the Adops team.
